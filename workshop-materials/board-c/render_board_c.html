Peer test and observation

Empirical protocol and observations from two 15-minute testing rounds with Team 7.

1. Task: [user goal without instructions]

"You just purchased a pair of Sony WH-1000XM4 headphones on sale at Saturn with a paper receipt. Log your new headphones into your wallet so you are protected against hardware defects, get alerted before statutory deadlines, and check your legal warranty rights."

2. Outcome: [unaided / with help / not completed]

• Round 1 (Initial wireframes): WITH HELP (38.0s)
Tester paused looking for automated camera OCR scan, typed slowly with hesitation over researching legal warranty lengths, bypassed receipt upload, and suffered confusion over separate warranty tabs.

• Round 2 (Revised wireframes): UNAIDED (11.4s) — 70% Speedup
In-memory brand autocomplete pre-filled Sony defaults in <10ms, tester snapped receipt attachment immediately beside Item Name, tapped Save, and viewed active BGB § 477 protection badge in one fluid flow.

3. What happened? / Why does it matter? [Hesitations, errors, assistance]
• Hesitation (Typing & Legal Dread): Tester froze on Brand input expecting OCR and dreaded researching German warranty law.
↳ Why it matters: Typing friction and legal dread destroy onboarding. Prefix-trie autocomplete pre-fills legal defaults in <10ms with zero token burn.
• Error (Receipt Attachment Bypassed): Tester tapped "Save" before attaching receipt because camera was below optional notes.
↳ Why it matters: Missing receipt proof invalidates future claims under BGB § 437. Repositioned camera attach directly beside Item Name with GoBD SHA-256 hash.
• Confusion (Statutory vs Commercial Tabs): Split tabs for guarantee vs warranty caused uncertainty over remedy in month 8.
↳ Why it matters: Legal jargon causes decision paralysis. Unified 24-month visual timeline with Month 12 Beweislastumkehr (§ 477) clarifies rights.
4. Change / priority / reason [Revisions based on observations]
1. In-Memory Brand Autocomplete (Prefix-Trie) [HIGH / Story 1]: Auto-fills category and 24m statutory defaults (BGB § 438) upon typing 2 characters; reduced capture time from 38.0s to 11.4s with zero cloud API token cost.
2. Repositioned Receipt Attachment [HIGH / Story 2]: Relocated camera button directly into the primary 3-field container beside Item Name; features client-side downsampling (<1.5MB) and GoBD-compliant SHA-256 pre-encryption hash stamp.
3. Unified Timeline & 1-Tap Claim Generator [MEDIUM / Stories 4 & 5]: Replaced split tabs with continuous 24-month progress bar highlighting Month 12 cliff (BGB § 477 Abs. 1) and 1-tap formal Mängelrüge generator (§ 437).
